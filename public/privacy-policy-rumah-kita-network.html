Privacy Policy - Rumah Kita Network

Last updated: May 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rumah Kita Network collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use the Rumah Kita Network mobile application.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data (for example: PPPoE username).
  • Profile data (for example: name, phone number, service address if available).
  • Billing and transaction data (invoices and payment confirmation data).
  • Technical data required for app functionality (for example: device identifier if provided).

2. How We Use Data

  • To authenticate users and maintain session security.
  • To provide customer dashboard and invoice information.
  • To process payment confirmation submitted through the app.
  • To provide customer support and service communication.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with third parties for advertising or third-party analytics purposes. Data may be shared with trusted service providers only when required to operate, secure, and improve service delivery, and subject to confidentiality obligations.

4. Data Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted transmission for production API communication.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as needed for service operation, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and fraud prevention.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data, subject to applicable laws and operational requirements.

To request deletion, send an email to support@rumahkitanet.id with subject: "Data Deletion Request - Rumah Kita Network".

7. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact:
Email: support@rumahkitanet.id

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
